# Student discount categories (search checklist)

Use this as a coverage map. For each item, **search the live official page** before listing it.
Offers change; never treat this file as a price list or a source of coupon codes.

## 1. Shopping & everyday retail

- Amazon Prime Student / Amazon student deals (country-specific)
- Apple Education / university store pricing
- Samsung, Best Buy, Target, Walmart student or .edu programs (where they exist)
- Clothing / outdoor brands with UNiDAYS, Student Beans, or SheerID
- Campus bookstore price-match or student days

## 2. Food & restaurants

- Chain restaurants with student ID or app student menus
- Campus dining guest / flex / late-night deals (school-specific pages)
- Delivery apps: student promos on DoorDash, Uber Eats, Grubhub (official app/partner pages only)
- Coffee chains with student loyalty or .edu signup

## 3. Grocery & household

- Grocery chains with student or college loyalty (region-specific)
- Warehouse clubs: student or young-adult memberships where offered
- Meal-kit or grocery-delivery student trials (official only)
- Campus farmers market or co-op member rates

## 4. Travel & transit

- Amtrak / rail student fares, regional transit student passes
- Airlines / buses with student or youth fares (e.g. StudentUniverse-style official partners)
- Hotels / Hostelworld student rates via verified partners
- Bike / scooter / Zipcar or campus carshare student plans
- ISIC / local student transit ID if relevant to the country

## 5. AI models, APIs & credits

- ChatGPT / OpenAI education or student plans (official OpenAI pages)
- Anthropic / Claude education or student offers if published
- Google Gemini / Google Cloud for education or students
- Microsoft Copilot / Azure for Students credits
- Cursor, GitHub Copilot (often via GitHub Education / Student Pack)
- Other model hosts with student, research, or education tiers (label the tier correctly)
- Startup / research credit programs (distinguish from pure student deals)

## 6. Software & creative tools

- GitHub Education / Student Developer Pack (umbrella for many tools)
- Microsoft 365 Education, Azure for Students
- Google Workspace for Education (when the school provides it)
- JetBrains, JetBrains Academy products
- Adobe Creative Cloud education
- Notion, Figma, Canva, Miro education plans
- Overleaf, Grammarly, Notion AI, or writing tools with student pricing
- Antivirus / VPN / password managers with student plans
- Statistical / STEM tools: MATLAB, Mathematica, LabVIEW (often via campus license — check school first)

## 7. Cloud, hosting & developer

- AWS Educate / AWS free tier guidance (official)
- Google Cloud / Azure student credits
- DigitalOcean, Railway, Render, Netlify, Vercel education or student trials (official)
- Domain registrars with student credits (e.g. via Student Pack partners)
- Education IDE / design tool free tiers via Student Pack

## 8. Campus life & academics

- University-negotiated discounts listed on student affairs / "student perks" pages
- Textbook: publisher rentals, Inclusive Access, OpenStax, library course reserves
- Printing / makerspace / gym student rates
- Museum, cinema, local attractions with student ID

## 9. Banking, phones & insurance (careful)

- Student checking / credit-builder products (informational only; no applications submitted for them)
- Phone plans with student discounts
- Renter or health insurance student rates via school portals
- Never collect SSN, bank passwords, or ID photos in chat; send them to the official site / secure browser flow

## Priority order for a broad "what am I missing?" ask

1. GitHub Student Pack (if CS / any major that uses tools)
2. Amazon Prime Student (or local equivalent)
3. Microsoft / Google education + campus licenses
4. Major AI student or education plans the student named
5. Food / grocery / local retail for their city
6. Travel pass if they mentioned trips
7. Everything else from the lists above as a PDF if needed
